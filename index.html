AfterSound AI is AI-powered music growth. It helps a label plan a release, approve what gets spent, and read the results without treating an estimate as a fact.

A desk for the next release, not a promise of virality.

Independent labels already rebuild the same setup, creative notes, and spreadsheets for every song. AfterSound keeps that work in one workspace: the song, the approved files, the test, the person who said yes, and the report that came back.

During the pilot, media stays in the customer’s own ad account. Any software or service fee is invoiced manually. This product does not take payment, hold a wallet, or raise a budget on its own.

What we will not say

  • No guaranteed streams, saves, or return on ad spend.
  • A click out to a streaming service is not a verified stream.
  • Simulated delivery is labeled. It is not a live TikTok campaign.
  • If a report is late, the last good import stays visible and spend advice turns off.

How a release moves

  1. 01

    Intake

    Song, dates, territories, goal, budget, and the rights on each file.

  2. 02

    Suggestions

    Editable tests from audio that was actually processed. A person picks the timestamp otherwise.

  3. 03

    Approval

    Creative, permissions, goal, dates, and spend are frozen into a version.

  4. 04

    Execution

    Manual checklist, or a clearly labeled simulated TikTok adapter. A click is not a launch.

  5. 05

    Results

    Imported numbers keep their source, currency, window, and gaps. Unknown is not zero.

Who it is for

Teams that run more than one release and already have a promotion budget. The first workspace is a private pilot: an owner, an operator, an approver, and a viewer. A self-serve artist product waits until this loop is reliable.
